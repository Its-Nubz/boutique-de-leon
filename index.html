FAQ

A few things to know.

Can I purchase directly from the website?

No. BoutiqueDeLeon.com is a catalog and inquiry site. Products are not purchased through online checkout.

Does an item shown online guarantee availability?

No. Inventory can change quickly. Please inquire to confirm current availability.

Can I request an item that isn't listed?

Yes. Use the Request an Item form to tell us what you're looking for.
